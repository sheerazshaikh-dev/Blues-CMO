What we're building

Know which ads make you money, and stop losing jobs you already paid for.

Blues CMO is like having a marketing chief who watches every lead, every call and every invoice for you. It plugs into the tools you already use and tells you in plain English what's working, what's wasting money, and what your office should fix today.

YOU'LL KNOW

Which ads bring in paying jobs

Not clicks or calls. Real jobs, real invoices, real money in the bank.

YOU'LL CATCH

Money slipping through the cracks

Leads nobody called back, finished jobs never billed, leads you paid for but shouldn't have.

YOU'LL GET

A clear to-do list

Your office knows what to fix every morning. You know where to spend next month.

$3.99 $2.23

We tested it on a Houston plumber. Their report said every $1 on Google Local Services Ads brought back $3.99. The real number was $2.23, and 62 finished jobs had never been billed. That gap is what Blues CMO finds.
